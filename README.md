# Economic Analysis of Germany and Neighboring Countries

This project delves into the economic landscape of Germany and its neighboring countries through a comprehensive dataset encompassing key economic indicators. As someone with a background in financial technology, the aim is to leverage data-driven approaches to analyze and interpret economic trends prevalent in the region.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset provides a holistic snapshot of essential economic metrics, including Gross Domestic Product (GDP) in current and constant prices, GDP per capita, inflation rates measured by the Consumer Price Index (CPI), and the breakdown of gross value added by sector (agriculture, industry, services). These indicators offer insights into the economic health, growth trajectory, and sectoral composition of each country, facilitating a nuanced understanding of regional economic dynamics.

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Through exploratory data analysis (EDA), this project aims to uncover patterns, correlations, and anomalies within the dataset. By asking pertinent questions and conducting thorough analysis, we seek to extract valuable insights into:

     i) Trends and fluctuations in GDP over time for each country
    ii) Comparative analysis of GDP per capita among the countries
   iii) Inflation dynamics and their implications for financial markets
    iv) Sectoral contributions to GDP and shifts in sectoral composition over time
     v) Comparative advantages and structural changes within the economies
